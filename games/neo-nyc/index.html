GANG WAR · RED vs BLUE vs MILITARY

THE CITY IS AT WAR

  1. RED vs BLUE: two biker gangs fight for NEO NYC. Last gang riding wins.
  2. You have 3 lives. Get killed 3 times and you join the MILITARY, hunting the gangs you rode with.
  3. ACT I is a street fight: knives, pipes, chains, spears. ACT II: guns hit the streets and the Military moves in for real.
  4. Somewhere in the city hides THE PILL. After the first minute it starts to ping on the map. Whoever takes it becomes S.T.EVE, a superpower.
  5. When one gang is wiped out comes THE SWITCH: every rider left becomes a W.E.S. with a railgun.
  6. S.T.EVE wins by charging the MEGA BLAST. The MILITARY wins by crushing and containing the threat. The winning gang wins by stopping it for good.

Tips: ride together, because linked teammates hit harder and heal. Grab ★ special bikes. Take the SKYWAY (you can shoot down, they can't shoot up) or drop into the TUNNELS. Mind the trains and the harbor.

Full rules
You ride for RED against BLUE, 6 riders each. Every biker has 3 lives; lose them and you fight for the MILITARY. Every death sends one more soldier (and sometimes a jeep) into the city.
Knock them off, then finish them. Hits and hard crashes throw you off the bike; you keep your knife plus one item, the rest scatters. You only die on foot.
Nobody shows on the map unless they're fighting. Hit a pedestrian or a car and you're WANTED; at ★★★ you can't shake it.
THE PILL is hidden somewhere in the city. ACT I is a street fight: knives, pipes, chains and spears (+1 to +3 versions hit harder). After ~2½ minutes ACT II brings the guns and the full Military. The cyan SKYWAY is an elevated freeway: ride up a ramp at either end to race above the traffic (bikes can fly off the side and land fine): from up there you can shoot down at the street, but nobody on the ground can shoot up at you (helicopters can). Jump off the side if you dare. Orange ▼ TUNNEL portals drop you underground to cross town unseen, through an underground roundabout. Five ★ special bikes hide around the city: JUGGERNAUT (twin rockets, armored), GHOST (bullets pass through at speed), SKYRIDER (flies), RAZOR (blades shred anyone you pass), STROBE (invisible to radar). Nobody knows where the pill is for the first minute; then it starts calling out on the map: 2 pings a minute, then 4, then 8. Whoever finds it, from any side, becomes S.T.EVE: alone against everyone, with a beam that grows with power and right-click powers (explode, teleport, mega bomb teleport, NOVA). Kill S.T.Eve and the pill hides again. S.T.Eve can hide too, until the threat level climbs (collapsing buildings is loud).
Ride together: teammates whose auras touch link up (glowing tether): each link gives +12% damage, -6% damage taken and +1.5 HP/s, up to 4. Your aura grows with your level. Bikers level up mostly by killing rival bikers (soldiers are worth a quarter). One rider per gang is a PILL SEEKER.
When one gang is wiped out comes THE SWITCH: every rider left becomes a W.E.S. with a railgun, the map reveals everyone, the Military throws a shrinking ring around S.T.Eve and can only weaken it (VULNERABLE), and S.T.Eve races to charge the MEGA BLAST with kills (a W.E.S. is worth 3) before the ring locks it down.
Friendly fire is on at 25%.
