Privacy Policy

Monster Pizza arcade games, including the Monster Hockey: Ice Scream! app. Last updated October 25, 2026.

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The games don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or anything else about you.

High scores

If you get a high score, you can enter three letters (like "ABC"). Those initials, the score and the time are saved to a shared scoreboard (Google Firebase) so other players can see the top scores. Don't use your real name. Your device also remembers your own scores and settings locally.

Online play (Monster Hockey)

When you host or join an online game, a short room code and the technical details the two devices need to connect are stored briefly on Google Firebase. The two devices then connect directly to each other, which means each device can see the other's internet address, as with any online game. Nothing about you is kept.

Website visits

The Monster Pizza website counts page visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. The app doesn't use it.

Kids

The games are made to be family friendly and don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
